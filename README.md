# Forever Dungeon Routes

A dungeon map with routes for **World of Warcraft: Forever**, for all 34 dungeons and seven classic raids:
ready routes for the 25 classic dungeons and the seven raids, maps by Santiago Reyes and routes for all 9 new Forever
dungeons (Blackmaw Hold since 1.1.0 with all six of its levels), boss order and notes in every language of the game,
bosses that tick themselves off, an editor for your own routes in the spirit of
Mythic Dungeon Tools, and a route list where players put up their routes for everyone.

## Features

- Own map window with the game's dungeon map art. WoW: Forever has no dungeon maps of its own, but
  the art files are still in the game client; the addon draws them itself. No other addon needed.
- Standard routes for all 25 classic dungeons. The line color runs from the entrance to the last
  boss, arrows show the walking direction, dashed lines mark side paths. Since 1.3.8 a round sign
  with an arrow marks where the route starts, and the arrows are light with a dark edge, easy to see
  on every map. Since 1.3.0 the line can also have one colour of your choice: white, magenta or cyan
  (gear button, "Route line").
- The four new dungeons of WoW: Forever (The Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site:
  Wetlands, City of Dalaran): the game has no map art for them, so the addon shows the maps of
  Santiago Reyes (Atlas de Azeroth: Forever), used with his permission; City of Dalaran with the
  Underbelly as a second map level. Their rooms light up and their bosses tick themselves off.
  Each has a standard route, "Approximate (from guides)": since 1.1.0 the boss spots come from several
  sources that agree (guides with their maps, other addons, the game's NPC positions), the order from
  guides and runs, and the paths follow the floors on Santiago's maps. Every stop says what is known
  about it, also the name the game shows where it differs from the boss list (Infurnus is called
  Magmatus, The Butcher The Baron). Highland Horror (Excavation Site, only for an Alliance quest) and Lyn
  the Ignored (City of Dalaran, summoned) are optional stops; Arcanic Enigma is the boss list's Mana
  Elemental and ticks itself off. Mana Wraith is not a boss in any guide and has no stop.
- The five newer dungeons (The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, The
  Shaper's Terrace, levels 35 to 60, in the game client since November 7): all of them show the maps
  of Santiago Reyes (Alcaz Prison with both of its levels, The Drowned City since 1.2.7 with the sunken
  temple Ataal'Byzal as a second level, Blackmaw Hold since 1.1.0 with six: outside, the Festering Grotto
  and four levels below ground, where the game names no rooms; since 1.3.0 the map switches there to the
  level of a boss when its fight starts).
  Their bosses tick themselves off and their rooms light up where the game names them. Each has a route
  from run videos (the minimap during each fight, matched with the game's own minimap) and guides: The
  Drowned City since 1.0.7, Krol'dok Stronghold since 1.0.10, Alcaz Prison since 1.0.12, The Shaper's
  Terrace since 1.0.13 and Blackmaw Hold since 1.1.0. Bosses that need to be summoned (the console
  bosses of The Shaper's Terrace, with Power Crystals) or that many groups skip are optional stops.
- Seven classic raids (Molten Core to Naxxramas): the game's map art, rooms and areas, bosses that tick
  themselves off, and standard routes from the entrance from boss to boss over the walkable floors;
  Zul'Gurub and Ahn'Qiraj with two each ("Full clear" and "Efficient"). In the dungeon list they sit
  in the submenu "Raids". Of these, only Onyxia's Lair is part of WoW: Forever. It opens on December 9,
  together with the two new raids Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit, which are not in the addon yet. The
  other six are not open in WoW: Forever; their maps come from the map files in the game client.
- First the game's own minimap pictures (since 1.3.7; the switch "Minimap | Map" in the upper left corner
  of the map, or "Map from the game's minimap" behind the gear button): every dungeon and raid put together
  from the pictures the minimap shows there, one map per level, with the same routes, rooms and notes, north
  at the top like the minimap. Where the minimap shows too little of the way (Ironclad Cove, the Wall Walk of
  Shadowfang Keep, the Temple Gates of Ahn'Qiraj) the drawn map stays.
- Numbered bosses in route order, rares (`R`), optional bosses (`+`) and notes (`!`) with keys,
  events and quest items. Blue squares switch to the next map level. The round `?` beside the
  crosshair on the map explains these signs. Where the drawn map of a dungeon
  comes from a later version of the game and marks a boss that WoW: Forever does not have there, a
  note says so.
- Where you are, as well as the game allows: WoW gives addons no position inside dungeons (since
  patch 7.1), so there is no arrow for you or your group. But the game names the room you are in
  (the text above the minimap). The addon knows where these rooms lie: the name of your room stands
  below the map ("You are in: ..."), and the map follows you to the level of your room. Where the game
  gives a room the same name on two levels (Uldaman's Hall of the Crafters on the way down to Archaedas),
  the map shows the level of the next open boss. If you like,
  the room also glows softly on the map (gear button, "Rooms glow on the map (yours and your
  group's)", off at first; big rooms such as the Chapel Gardens glow faint and still instead of
  pulsing). Between named rooms the map follows your run: after a boss kill it shows the level of
  the next open boss, and when a boss fight starts the level of that boss.
- Your group on the map: the others in your group who also use the addon show as a small blue number
  in the room the game names for them, with a legend in the lower left corner (number, name, room;
  grey when they are on another level or the game names no room), so nothing else covers the map. On
  at first (gear button, "Show your group on the map (players with this addon)"); with "Rooms glow on
  the map" their rooms glow blue as well. Ticks by hand reach the whole group, and Alt-click shows a
  stop to all.
- Stop list with checkmarks per character. Bosses tick themselves off when the game reports the
  kill; rares without a boss fight are ticked by hand. "New run" clears the checkmarks, an old run
  is cleared after three hours, and after a reset of the dungeon they start over: when the game
  reports the reset (the addon passes it on to the group) or when a boss that already died is
  fought again.
- The way to the dungeon: outside, a click on "Entrance: <zone>" sets a map pin at the entrance that
  the game tracks, so the arrow on the screen leads there.
- Dungeons for your level: the dungeon list colours the names by your level, like quests.
- Run time from entering the dungeon to the last boss, your best time and your last runs per
  dungeon.
- Small map when you enter a dungeon: slightly transparent at the edge of the screen, it follows
  your run like the big map. Drag it by its title bar, resize it at its lower right corner; "+"
  makes it the full route map, X closes it, and it closes when you leave. The gear button chooses
  what shows on entering a dungeon: the small map, the route map or nothing. Since 1.3.9 "Map
  background" (gear button, Opacity) makes it see-through down to the route, for example to lay
  the route over your own minimap; the minimap stays centred on you, so drag the map along.
- Small display in dungeons (optional): dungeon, bosses down, run time, the next boss with its room
  and your room in a small box, for when the map stays closed.
- Route from the last run: the addon notes where the bosses fell; one click makes a route of it.
- Route editor: paths, side paths, stops and notes; drag to move, undo. Editing a standard route
  creates a copy.
- Share routes with your group or your guild in game: everyone with the addon gets the route
  offered and takes it over with one click. Post the boss order in the group chat for players
  without the addon, or share routes as text (export and import).
- Route list: players put their routes up for everyone, a click takes a route over, and good routes
  get recommended. The list comes from your guild, your parties and, if you turn it on, the server
  channel FDRoutes.
- Four window styles: gilded (the default: a beveled gold frame on a dark ground, with a title plate,
  the map levels as tabs, gold-framed buttons and a footer), dark, parchment (light like the map art)
  and classic (the game's gold frame).
- Button at the minimap (right-click for a few switches), an entry in broker bars (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar,
  ElvUI datatexts and the like), slash command and key binding.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese (simplified and
  traditional). Names of bosses, dungeons, map levels and rooms come from the game in the language
  of your client; the translations of the addon's own texts are welcome to be corrected (issues).
  Since 1.1.0 the notes on the routes and maps are in every language of the game too, with the names
  the game uses.

## Install

On [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-dungeon-routes), or with the CurseForge
app: search for "Forever Dungeon Routes".

By hand:

1. Download `ForeverDungeonRoutes-<version>.zip` from the [releases](../../releases).
2. Unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` (the beta used `_classic_beta_`).
   The folder must be named `ForeverDungeonRoutes`.

## Use

- The button at the minimap or `/fdr` opens or closes the map. Drag the button to move it around
  the minimap; right-click it for the small display and the route list channel. There is also a
  key binding (Key Bindings, section AddOns).
- With a display addon for LibDataBroker (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, ElvUI datatexts and the like) the addon
  also shows up in its bar: a click and a right-click work there as on the minimap button. The display brings
  LibDataBroker along; the addon does not ship it.
- Choose the dungeon and the map level in the header. Inside a dungeon the window opens on the
  level of the room you are in, else of the next open boss.
- Mouse wheel zooms, dragging moves the map, right-click zooms out. After you choose a level by
  hand the map stays there (a boss fight that starts more than half a minute later shows the level
  of that boss); the crosshair button shows where you are (your room, else the next open boss) and
  lets the map follow you again. The `?` beside it explains the signs on the map. Inside a dungeon it is there also while the map
  shows another one, and a click finds the dungeon and its wing anew (handy after loading the
  addon inside a dungeon).
- The padlock beside the `?` locks the window and the small map: they stay where they are, keep their
  size, and Escape no longer closes the window (the X, `/fdr` and the key still do). Click it again to
  unlock.
- The switch "Minimap | Map" in the upper left corner of the map, in the window and on the small map, changes
  between the game's minimap pictures (shown first since 1.3.7) and the drawn map (the same as "Map from the
  game's minimap" behind the gear button). The minimap pictures look like the minimap in your game, which makes
  it easier to see where you are on the route. Both views show north at the top, as the minimap does: on the
  levels of the new dungeons where Santiago's map is turned against north, the map turns it, and the route with
  it, so switching keeps everything in place.
- Your own marker: Shift-click on the map puts a green marker there, for example where you are. Drag it
  along as you go, right-click removes it; a new run takes it away. Only you see it. WoW gives addons no
  position inside dungeons, so this is the way to keep track of yourself on the route. Inside a dungeon a tip at the
  top of the map tells the keys until you set your first marker or click the tip away.
- Bosses tick themselves off when they die. A click on a boss on the map or in the list ticks it
  off by hand, for your whole group. Shift-click a list entry to show it on the map, Alt-click a
  stop to show it to your group (a ring on their map for half a minute).
- In a group, the others show on the map with a number in the room the game names for them and a
  legend with their names. This needs the addon on their side too. With "Rooms glow on the map"
  (gear button, off at first) their rooms light up blue, like your own in gold.
- Click the route name to choose a route, create a new one, copy, rename, delete or import.
  "Route from the last run" makes an own route of your last run in this dungeon: the bosses where
  they fell (in the middle of the room the game named, else where the standard route has them) and
  the path through the rooms. The submenu "Share" holds the ways to pass a route on:
  - "Send to group" (or `/fdr send`) and "Send to guild": players with the addon are asked whether
    to take the route over; nothing changes without their yes. A standard route travels as a short
    hint, an own route in small pieces (up to about half a minute for the largest ones). During
    boss fights the game holds addon messages back; sending then waits until the fight is over.
  - "Post the boss order in the group chat": "Deadmines: 1 Rhahk'Zor, R Miner Johnson, 2 ...",
    as plain text for everyone. Outside a group only you see it.
  - "Export route": the route as text to copy.
  - "In the route list" (own routes): the route goes in the route list, where every player with
    the addon can take it over.
- "Route list" in the same menu: the routes others put up for the shown dungeon, with their author
  and number of stops, recommended ones first. A click takes one over; a route taken over from
  the list can be recommended once ("Recommend this route" in the route menu), and its author's
  addon counts it. "Refresh the list" asks your guild and your party, and, where you turned on
  "Route list over the server channel" behind the gear button, the players in the chat channel
  FDRoutes; only then does the addon join this channel, and it hides it from your chat (off at first
  since 1.3.6: a joined channel takes a channel number and can move your own channels). Blizzard lets
  addons send no hidden messages to a chat channel, so the request there is a short visible line
  ("FDR:Q") that the addon hides, and the answers come by whisper. The route itself comes by whisper
  from its author or from a player who took it over.
- Above the stop list: the level of the dungeon in the colour of your level, the bosses down (the
  same count as in the small display; the mouse on it shows the optional and rare stops you ticked
  off besides; in the style gilded it stands on the head line of the stops), the run time and your
  best time (the mouse on them shows your last ten runs), and outside the dungeon its entrance. A
  click on the entrance sets the map pin.
- The gear button holds the settings: stop list, rooms glow on the map, your group on the map,
  map from the game's minimap, tick off bosses automatically, what shows on entering a dungeon (small map, route map or
  nothing), button at the minimap, small display in dungeons, route list over the server channel,
  route line (width, colour, arrows), text size of the stop list, window style (gilded, dark, parchment,
  classic), opacity of the window and, since 1.3.9, of the map background on its own (the map pictures
  and the ground behind them; route, stops and signs stay).
- With a controller (the game's controller mode) the menus of the addon show as a list of its own: the game steers
  its own menus there itself and blocks them when an addon opens them. A submenu opens in place with a line back at
  the top ("< ..."); a click beside the list, Escape or a second click on the same button closes it. From a button
  in the lower half of the screen (a broker bar at the bottom) the list opens upwards.

Stratholme, Maraudon and Dire Maul North come with two standard routes each (living and undead
side, purple and orange side, full clear and tribute run). In Stratholme the map picks the side by itself: on
Crusader's Square the living side, on The Gauntlet the undead side, and a boss fight of the other side switches over
as well. Own routes are never switched.

## Own routes

"Edit" on a standard route creates a copy, the standard route itself stays unchanged.
While editing:

- **Path / Side path**: left-click adds a point, right-click removes the last one, shift-click
  starts a new piece, ctrl-click selects an existing piece.
- **Stop**: left-click places a stop. On a stop: click for options (rename, note, kind, boss in the
  game, order), drag to move, right-click to delete. A stop named like one of the dungeon's bosses
  is linked to that boss and ticks itself off.
- **Note**: left-click places a note. On a note: click to edit, drag to move, right-click to delete.
- **Undo** reverts the last change, **Done** ends editing. Right-drag moves the map while editing.

Your own routes work for every dungeon: choose the dungeon and "New route".

## Commands

`/fdr` shows or hides the map, `/fdr send` sends the active route to your group, `/fdr way` sets the
map pin at the entrance of the shown dungeon, `/fdr reset` clears the checkmarks and starts a new
run, `/fdr pos` prints the position data and the room the game reports (helps with bug reports),
`/fdr menu` shows the menus as in controller mode also with the mouse, until the next `/reload`.
`/fdr report` (or "Report a problem or idea" at the bottom of the gear menu) shows a text to copy
into a comment on CurseForge or an issue on GitHub: the versions of the addon and the game, the
language of the client, the map on screen and the settings that change it, nothing about you.

## How the map works

The classic dungeon and raid maps are Blizzard's own map tiles; which tiles make up which map level
comes from Blizzard's map tables (`Data/Floors.lua`). Since 1.3.3 the addon brings these tiles along
(`tiles/`, copied unchanged from the game files) and uses them on every client: the Russian, Spanish
(Mexico) and Italian game clients do not have them and showed the maps green. Inside dungeons the game gives addons
no position, only the name of the room you are in. Where the rooms lie (`Data/Rooms.lua`) is
generated from the game's building files: each named part of a dungeon building with its outline,
placed on the map level where the game's own map shows it. Rooms come with all dungeons except
Shadowfang Keep, Stormwind Stockade, Razorfen Kraul, Zul'Farrak and Dire Maul (there only the entrance
of East and West); the game names no rooms there. Between rooms the map follows the boss kills the
game reports. In Scarlet Monastery and Dire Maul the door you came through tells the wing as soon
as you enter: the addon notes where you stood outside before the loading screen and takes the wing
whose portal is there (the portals of the classic world, from the game's own trigger table of
Classic Era), else the game's map of the wing. There and in Blackrock Spire a kill also tells which
wing you are in (Lower or Upper Spire), in Scarlet Monastery and Upper Blackrock Spire already the
first room.

Upper Blackrock Spire has no map level in today's map tables. Its stadium, furnace and throne room
are on Blizzard's old seventh map level of Blackrock Spire, which is still in the WoW: Forever
client; Dragonspire Hall, the Hall of Binding and the Rookery are drawn on the map levels of the
Lower Spire, and the addon shows them there under their own names.

The entrances (`Data/Entrances.lua`) are WoW: Forever's own map points where it has them, else the
positions of Blizzard's table of dungeon entrances, placed on Forever's zone maps.

For the new Forever dungeons there is no map art in the game. All nine show Santiago's maps
(`maps/*.blp`, `Data/Pictures.lua`), fitted to the game's coordinates with the game's minimap pictures.
Their rooms are the areas and named building parts the game reports there. The game has no entrance data for these dungeons.

Every map level shows first the game's own minimap pictures (since 1.3.7; `Data/Minimaps.lua`, by file
ID like the other map art): the pictures of the building parts the game puts on the level (where WoW:
Forever rebuilt a dungeon, the new parts take the level of the old ones in the same place), outdoors those
of the terrain tiles, turned and put together on the same spot as the map art, so routes and rooms fit both.
Levels where these pictures show less than 60 % of the standard route keep the map art. On every level with
Santiago's map the same pictures are also laid out north up over a rectangle of the world (`DR.MinimapArtNorth`:
the whole drawn map where it is turned only a little, else around the building, the route and the rooms; for The
Underbelly, which has no minimap pictures in the game, the rectangle alone). Both views show that rectangle: the
minimap pictures from there and Santiago's map turned through its texture coordinates (the space around it in
`maps/*.blp` is clear since 1.3.7), with routes, rooms and markers turned the same way when drawn, while
everything saved keeps the coordinates of the drawn map.
