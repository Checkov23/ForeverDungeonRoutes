Map tiles in this folder (<file ID>.blp): the maps of the classic dungeons and raids of
World of Warcraft, art by Blizzard Entertainment, copied unchanged from the game files of
WoW: Forever (build 1.60.1.70959). The Russian, Spanish (Mexico) and Italian game clients
do not have them and show these maps green, so the addon brings them along and uses them
on every client.

They are not covered by the MIT license of this addon; all rights stay with Blizzard
Entertainment.
